About us

Clarity, in a system built on paperwork

Startup Resources Centre exists so that no eligible entrepreneur is turned away by confusing eligibility criteria or scattered information. We connect people to the right scheme, and help them through the documentation.

Aim

Make government support discoverable and usable for every entrepreneur, regardless of scale or location.

Vision

A Telangana where every eligible business accesses the funding and recognition it qualifies for.

Values

Clarity, Accessibility, Transparency, Empowerment — in every interaction, every document, every referral.
